# Community conduct

Be respectful and specific. Critique behavior and code with reproducible evidence.
Welcome questions and contributions from people with different backgrounds and
experience. Give others room to correct mistakes.

Harassment, threats, personal attacks, discriminatory abuse, doxxing and sharing
private account data are not accepted. Do not pressure contributors to test on
servers without permission or to make unsupported compatibility claims.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, close discussions or restrict
participation. Use GitHub's reporting tools for abuse involving private information
or threats. Report software vulnerabilities through [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
